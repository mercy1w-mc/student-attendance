ACADEMIC ELIGIBILITY

Attendance Calculator

Calculate eligibility separately for every student's course enrolment.

Set the required attendance percentage and calculate eligibility.

Formula: (sessions attended ÷ total sessions) × 100. A student meets the requirement when their percentage is equal to or greater than the selected threshold.

How eligibility works

Example: 8 sessions attended out of 10 sessions.

Attendance = (8 ÷ 10) × 100 = 80%.

If the requirement is 75%, the student meets the requirement.

If the requirement is 85%, the student is below the requirement.
